DISPUTE TYPES YOU'LL ENCOUNTER

Each dispute tests a different misconception about data ownership. Watch for: origin ≠ owner (who generates data is not who governs it) · usage ≠ owner (who reads the data is not who owns it) · lineage pipe ≠ owner (passing through a system is not stewardship) · regulatory override (GDPR-regulated data always falls under Compliance, regardless of origin).

REAL-WORLD TRANSLATION
You ruled on four data ownership conflicts — using stewardship chains, lineage pipelines, and classification flags to determine who is truly accountable for each dataset.

In the real world this is Data Governance: ownership is not determined by who generates data, who stores it, or whose name appears in the filename. It is determined by the Data Steward — the accountable person recorded in the governance registry. A file called Marketing_Analytics.dat may be owned by IT. A dataset stored in Finance may be governed by Compliance. The metadata is the ground truth — not the folder it lives in.
